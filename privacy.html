Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Ascent Method ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Ascent Method application ("App").

By using Ascent Method, you agree to the collection and use of information as described below.

1Our Philosophy

We believe fitness should be accessible, supportive, and free from unnecessary barriers. That includes being clear and honest about how your data is used. We collect only what we need to make the App helpful.

2Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details (such as email or username)
  • Fitness preferences and goals
  • Workout history and training inputs
  • Optional feedback or messages you submit

Fitness & usage data

  • Workouts completed or logged
  • Training volume, intensity, and load
  • App interaction data (features used, timestamps)

Device & technical data

  • Device type and operating system
  • App performance and error logs
  • Anonymous usage analytics

We do not collect precise location data or require sensitive personal details.

3How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Generate personalized workout suggestions
  • Adapt training based on previous sessions and load
  • Improve App performance and features
  • Maintain account security
  • Communicate important updates or support messages

AI-generated recommendations rely on the data you provide.

4Artificial Intelligence & Data

Ascent Method uses artificial intelligence (AI) to analyze training patterns and suggest workouts.

  • AI outputs are generated based on your inputs
  • Your data may be processed to improve personalization
  • AI responses are not reviewed by humans in real time
  • AI is designed to support your fitness journey — not make decisions for you

5Subscriptions & Billing

Ascent Method offers auto-renewable subscriptions processed through Apple's App Store. Subscription billing, renewal, and cancellation are managed by Apple. We do not directly collect or store your payment information. Subscription status is maintained through Apple's StoreKit system.

6Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data only:

  • With trusted service providers who help operate the App
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the safety, rights, or integrity of Ascent Method or its users

All partners are required to protect your data.

7Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including secure storage systems, access controls, and industry-standard safeguards. However, no system is 100% secure. You use the App at your own risk.

8Your Choices

You may:

  • Update or correct your information within the App
  • Stop using the App at any time
  • Request account deletion by contacting us at ascentfitness15@gmail.com

Some data may be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

9Children's Privacy

Ascent Method is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

10Third-Party Services

Ascent Method relies on third-party services including Apple's App Store and StoreKit for subscription management, and cloud infrastructure providers for data storage. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties. Please review their policies separately.

11No Medical Data Handling

Ascent Method does not collect or store medical records, diagnoses, or health provider information. Fitness data is used only for general wellness guidance and is not treated as medical data.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted in the App. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
