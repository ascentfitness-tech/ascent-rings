Common questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID, not inside Ascent. Open your device Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Ascent Method. You can change or cancel your plan there at any time.

How does my program adapt?
Ascent reads your logged performance and session readiness after each workout and adjusts what comes next — load, volume, and progression. You earn each step up.

What equipment do I need?
Ascent has programs for three environments: bodyweight only, basic equipment (dumbbells and kettlebells), and a full commercial gym. You choose during setup.

I found a bug or something looks wrong.
Email us with your device model, what you were doing, and a screenshot if you have one. It helps us fix it faster.

Account & data

To request deletion of your account and associated training data, email us from the address tied to your account and we'll take care of it.
